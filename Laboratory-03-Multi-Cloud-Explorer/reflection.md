# ☁️ Mission Reflection

After completing this cloud computing mission, **Amazon Web Services (AWS)** is the cloud platform that impressed me the most. I was impressed by the wide variety of services it provides and its ability to support different organizations, from small startups to large enterprises. AWS also offers flexible and scalable resources that can be adjusted based on an organization's needs.

AWS, Microsoft Azure, and Google Cloud Platform (GCP) share many similar features. All three provide services such as virtual machines, storage, databases, networking, security, and identity management. They also allow organizations to increase or decrease their resources when needed, manage services through online consoles, and run applications without having to maintain all of the physical hardware themselves.

Business requirements play an important role when choosing a cloud platform because each organization has different goals, budgets, technologies, and workloads. For example, **Microsoft Azure** can be a suitable option for organizations that already rely heavily on Microsoft products. **Google Cloud Platform (GCP)** is a strong option for organizations working with Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, and Kubernetes. **AWS** can be suitable for businesses that require a wide range of cloud services along with flexible and scalable resources.

Working as a **Cloud Solutions Consultant** taught me that choosing a cloud provider is not only about selecting the most popular platform. I learned that it is important to consider the client's objectives, budget, current systems, workload requirements, and future plans before making a recommendation.

Lastly, my **GitHub Cloud Computing Portfolio** has become more complete and organized through this mission. It now includes cloud research, platform comparisons, client recommendations, service mappings, and Linux server investigation results. Completing this mission improved my understanding of the major cloud platforms and gave me practical knowledge that I can apply to future IT projects.
